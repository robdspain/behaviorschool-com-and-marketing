# BehaviorSchool SEO Measurement Gate

## Purpose

Use first-party search data to decide which SEO pages should be improved, differentiated, consolidated, redirected, or removed from search. Do not make intent or content changes based on public search snapshots alone.

## Required Google Search Console windows

- July 1-19, 2026
- July 20, 2026
- July 21-31, 2026
- August 1-14, 2026
- Matched weekday and weekly comparisons where available

## Required dimensions and metrics

Date, query, page, device, country, search appearance, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position.

Derived fields must identify July 20 improvements, post-July-20 declines, query-to-page switching, branded versus non-branded queries, intent cluster, and sample-size confidence.

## Decision gate

Do not change page ownership, canonical targets, redirects, or exam-page content strategy until the report identifies the affected queries and pages. A row with low impressions may be exploratory but cannot decide consolidation by itself.

## Intent clusters

- Quick free diagnostic
- Full timed mock exam
- General BCBA practice exam
- BCBA exam preparation
- School BCBA practice
- Functional behavior assessment
- FBA decision making
- Transformation Program

## Cross-engine monitoring

Verify Bing Webmaster Tools and record whether historical data is available. Submit the sitemap and export Bing query/page performance when available. Maintain a fixed panel of approximately 25 searches across Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, and Yahoo using the same location, device, and query wording. Use Bing as the measurable proxy for DuckDuckGo's traditional web results, while keeping manual checks for the other engines.

## Business funnel

Track organic impression, organic click, free exam start, exam completion, account creation, checkout start, and purchase completion by landing page and intent cluster. Do not add user counts, testimonials, outcome statistics, scores, or ratings unless the exact source data has been verified and approved.

## Observation rule

After the approved search-appearance patch, collect at least four weeks of data. The post-observation review may prepare a page-level change set, but it must not publish, push, or deploy changes automatically. Every recommendation must distinguish observed data from a hypothesis and identify the metric that will determine whether the change worked.
