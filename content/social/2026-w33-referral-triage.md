At the start of the school year, behavior referrals often arrive as labels:

"Defiant."

"Aggressive."

"Needs an FBA."

Before choosing an assessment path, I want the team to answer three questions:

1. What did the student do that someone could observe and count?
2. Is there an immediate safety, disciplinary, or procedural action to address?
3. What decision does the team need the assessment to support?

Then assign an owner and a review date.

That creates a usable starting point for consultation, record review, direct observation, additional data, or a formal assessment process. The correct next step still depends on the student, the team, and local requirements.
